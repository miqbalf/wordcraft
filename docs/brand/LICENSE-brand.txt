What you may do

miqbalf lets you use the udinCraft Marks, free of charge, without exclusivity and until this
permission is withdrawn, only:

  1. inside unmodified copies of this repository; and
  2. in this udinCraft build of WordCraft, its documentation and the builds miqbalf publishes, to show

On these conditions

  - Use the files as they are. Do not redraw, recolor, distort, animate or take apart the marks,
    and do not merge them with other names or artwork.
  - Do not use them in a way that suggests miqbalf or udinCraft made, backs or supports anything
    else, such as another product, service, fork or modified version.
  - Do not put "udinCraft" or the marks in the name, logo, icon, domain, account name, store
    listing or advertising of anything else.

Forks and modified versions

If you publish or hand out a modified version of this repository, or something built from it,
take the udinCraft Marks out (or put your own in their place) and do not call your work
udinCraft. You may say in plain text that it is based on udinCraft WordCraft. A fork used only to
send changes back here may keep the marks.
